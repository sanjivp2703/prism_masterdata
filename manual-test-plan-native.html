Timing. Prism looks for changes every minute, but only standardizes and exports at the 10-minute marks (:00, :10, :20 …), and only while a pipeline's update window is open. Deletes are handled within a minute. A top-of-hour pass reconciles everything as a safety net. After inserting rows, expect the export to catch up at the next 10-minute mark, not instantly.
Weekends and evenings. A pipeline on the default Time window only standardizes Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM in your timezone. Outside that, inserted rows are detected and queued but nothing goes out — which looks like "the export isn't changing". Before any test in section E, check the CUSTOMER_ORDERS card says 24/7; if it says Mon–Fri, do C6 first.
Cost. The app bills while running. The last test stops it. Don't leave it running overnight unless a test needs it.
